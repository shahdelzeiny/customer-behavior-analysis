# Amazon Fine Food Reviews Analysis using NLP

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer reviews from the Amazon Fine Food Reviews dataset using Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques. The goal is to extract meaningful insights from customer feedback, identify common discussion topics, and measure similarity between reviews to better understand customer behavior and preferences.

---

## Business Problem

E-commerce platforms receive thousands of customer reviews every day. Manually analyzing these reviews is difficult and time-consuming.

This project aims to:

* Understand customer preferences and concerns.
* Discover common topics discussed in reviews.
* Identify similar customer experiences.
* Generate business insights that support decision-making.

---

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Amazon Fine Food Reviews

The dataset contains:

* Customer review text
* Product ratings
* Product information
* User feedback data

---

## Project Pipeline

1. Data Understanding

3. Data Cleaning
4. Text Preprocessing

## Key Findings

### Customer Priorities

Customers mainly focus on:

* Taste and flavor
* Product quality
* Price and value
* Pet satisfaction

### Dominant Product Categories

* Coffee and beverages
* Snacks and sweets
* Pet food products

### Customer Behavior

Many reviews express similar experiences and opinions, indicating recurring patterns in customer feedback.

### Business Value

The extracted topics and similarity patterns can help companies:

* Improve products
* Detect customer pain points
* Enhance recommendation systems
* Better understand customer needs
